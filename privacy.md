# Offseason Tracker Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Offseason Tracker does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- **No accounts.** You never sign in or give your name or email address.
- **No tracking or analytics.** The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
- **On-device settings only.** Your favorite team, offseason start date and notification preference are stored only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app.
- **Public data requests.** The app downloads public baseball data (rosters, statistics, transactions) from the MLB Stats API and news headlines from the MLB Trade Rumors RSS feed. These requests contain no personal information. Those services may log standard request details (such as IP address) under their own privacy policies.
- **Notifications.** If you enable roster-move notifications, they are created on your device. No push-notification token is sent anywhere.
