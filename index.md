# Offseason Tracker

Offseason Tracker is an independent iPhone app for following your baseball team's offseason: the projected lineup and record, every roster move since the season ended, available free agents, and which teams are buying or selling at each position.

## FAQ

**How is the projected record calculated?** Each player's WAR from the last three seasons is weighted (5/4/3), regressed toward an average player and adjusted for age. The app builds a depth chart, and every WAR above the league-average roster counts as one win above 81.

**How current is the data?** Rosters, transactions and free agents update within minutes of being official. The app re-checks every 3 minutes while it's open.

**Where do rumors come from?** Headlines from the MLB Trade Rumors RSS feed, linked back to the original articles.
